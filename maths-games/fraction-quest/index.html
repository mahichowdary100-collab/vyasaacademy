🍕 Fraction Quest

The 3D Fraction Adventure — build, escape, battle, blitz & match

Five hands-on game modes for CBSE and ICSE Classes 6 to 10 — build fraction towers, balance equivalent fractions, simplify to simplest form, compare by cross-multiplication, compute, convert and race the clock. All answers are checked with exact fraction arithmetic.

How to Play

  1. Fraction Builder — choose a level (Class 6-7 up to Class 9-10). Tap cells, layers or symbols to construct, label, match and simplify fractions. Explanations follow every answer.
  2. Fraction Escape Room — each chamber has three locks with exact integer answers, then an exit code that is the sum of the three lock answers. Use a hint when stuck and read the worked explanation after any wrong attempt.
  3. Beat the Fraction Bot — you and the bot get the same question each round; the bot answers instantly. First to four correct answers wins. Defeat the Final Fraction Boss for a special achievement.
  4. Fraction Blitz — pick 30, 60 or 120 seconds. Type answers like 3/4, 1 3/4, 0.75 or 75% and press Enter. Press P to pause. Streaks boost your points.
  5. Fraction Match Arena — tap one fraction card, then its matching partner on the right. Correct pairs lock in green; a wrong guess is counted but the board stays up.

Press Esc to return home; press 1–4 to answer multiple-choice quickly. Progress and XP are stored privately on this device.
