Master perfect squares and square roots with Square & Root Ninja

Square & Root Ninja turns classwork revision into a fast, friendly battleground. Hunt perfect squares on number grids, crack square roots like √144 = 12, and complete square-number sequences such as 1, 4, 9, 16, … — all with instant feedback and step-by-step explanations. Every grid, question and sequence is generated fresh, so no two training sessions repeat.

Skills you will sharpen

  • A perfect square is n × n for a whole number n: 0, 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, …
  • √n is the non-negative number whose square is n. For example, √625 = 25 because 25 × 25 = 625.
  • Distinguish √n (the principal square root) from the equation x² = n, which has two solutions: +√n and −√n.
  • Squares of consecutive whole numbers form clear patterns (1, 4, 9, 16, 25, …) that reveal a single missing term.
  • Three difficulties, four play modes and three timed lengths build speed for real exam conditions.

Squares and square roots are core mental-maths skills across the school curriculum. Pair the game with our CBSE study material, level up with Class 8 Mathematics, or explore the other maths games we have built for students.
