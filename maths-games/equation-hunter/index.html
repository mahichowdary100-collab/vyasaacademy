Why It Helps

Practise Algebra with Equation Hunter

A quick, focused way to sharpen equation-solving skills.

The Equation Hunter game turns algebra practice into a fast mental challenge. Students are shown an equation such as 3x + 5 = 20 and must find the value of x before the timer runs out, earning points for every correct answer and bonus points for streaks.

From one-step to two-step equations

Questions are built backwards from a known solution, so every equation is guaranteed to have a clean integer answer. On the easy level students solve simple equations like x + 6 = 14; the medium level adds two-step forms like 3x - 4 = 20; and the hard level introduces bracket equations such as 2(x + 4) = 18 with the occasional negative answer.

Fit practice into the CBSE routine

Equation solving sits at the heart of the CBSE Mathematics curriculum from middle school onwards. Pair the game with our full CBSE study material, chapter tests for Class 9 Maths and Class 10 Maths, or explore more Maths games.
