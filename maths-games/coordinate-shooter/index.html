Why It Helps

Practise Coordinate Geometry with Coordinate Shooter

Read coordinates, locate points and explore all four quadrants on a real interactive plane.

The Coordinate Shooter game turns coordinate geometry practice into a fast hands-on challenge. In Find the Point mode a coordinate such as (4, 3) is shown and the student clicks its location on the plane. In Identify the Coordinate mode a point is highlighted and the student types its X and Y values.

Explore all four quadrants and the axes

Every target is placed exactly where its mathematical coordinate appears: x units right or left from the origin and y units up or down. On easy difficulty coordinates stay within -5 to +5, while medium and hard levels range from -10 to +10 and include points on the x-axis, the y-axis, the origin and all four quadrants.

A quick daily boost alongside CBSE study

Coordinate geometry is a core CBSE Mathematics topic from Class 9 onwards. Pair the game with our Class 10 Coordinate Geometry chapter notes, tests and quizzes, or explore more Maths games on Vyasa Academy.
