Why It Helps

Practise Mental Maths with the Speed Challenge

A quick, focused way to sharpen everyday arithmetic.

The Maths Speed Challenge is a timed mental arithmetic game for CBSE students who want to solve addition, subtraction, multiplication and division questions faster and more accurately. You choose your difficulty level, pick a time limit, and answer as many questions as you can before the timer runs out.

Build speed without sacrificing accuracy

Every correct answer earns points, and answer streaks unlock bonus points up to three and five in a row. The challenge rewards careful, quick calculation rather than guessing — and your best score, accuracy and streak are saved on your device so you can beat them in the next round.

Fit practice into the CBSE routine

A single 60-second round fits neatly into a study break. For deeper preparation, pair the game with our full CBSE study material, chapter tests for Class 9 Maths and Class 10 Maths, or explore more Maths games.
