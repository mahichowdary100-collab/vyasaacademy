Master statistics with Data Detective

Data Detective turns CBSE and ICSE statistics revision into a detective story. Practise reading bar charts, decoding pie charts, computing the mean, finding the median and mode, and interrogating frequency tables — all with instant feedback and step-by-step explanations. Every dataset is generated fresh so no two cases repeat.

What you will practise

  • Mean = sum of observations ÷ number of observations.
  • Median = the middle value after sorting (average of the two middles when n is even).
  • Mode = the most frequent value; datasets with no repeated value have no mode.
  • Pie sector angle = (category value ÷ total) × 360°.
  • Reading totals, differences, highest/lowest and averages from graphs and tables.

Statistics and data handling appear throughout the school curriculum. Practise alongside our Class 8 Mathematics and Class 9 Mathematics study material, then challenge yourself with more maths games.
