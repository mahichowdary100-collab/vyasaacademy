How to Play
  • Players take turns rolling the dice and moving that many squares.
  • Land on the bottom of a ladder and climb to the top.
  • Land on a snake’s head and slide down to its tail.
  • You must roll the exact number to land on square 100 — an overshoot keeps you in place.
  • The first player to reach square 100 wins.
  • Drag the board to rotate and use scroll or pinch to zoom.
