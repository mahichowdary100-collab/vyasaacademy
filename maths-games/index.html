About Our Games

Maths Learning Games for Students

Making everyday Mathematics practice feel like play.

Vyasa Academy's Maths Learning Games are designed to help CBSE students practise mathematical concepts through interactive challenges. Instead of drilling through long worksheets, students can pick a game that matches what they are learning — from mental arithmetic speed drills to algebra, geometry, coordinate graphs, statistics and probability — and improve through short, focused play sessions.

Games that complement the CBSE syllabus

Every game in our Maths learning arcade is planned around the concepts students meet in the CBSE curriculum. The same ideas behind our study notes and chapter tests appear inside the challenges, so classroom learning and game practice reinforce each other naturally.

Built for daily CBSE preparation

Whether a student is revising for a weekly test, preparing for board exams, or simply building speed and accuracy, the games will sit alongside the resources they already use — detailed notes, practice questions and chapter tests — to make CBSE Mathematics preparation more consistent and enjoyable.

The Maths Speed Challenge, Equation Hunter and Coordinate Shooter games are now live and free to play. More games are being prepared and will be added to each CBSE study material hub. In the meantime, explore the Maths resources below:
