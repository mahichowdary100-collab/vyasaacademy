Run a Business. Manage Your Money. Master Financial Maths.

How to Play

  1. Shop Manager — buy stock at cost, choose a selling price and an optional discount, then open the shop to serve daily customers. Customers are more likely to buy when your price is fair and your reputation is high. Finish each day with Close Day, which pays rent, staff and utilities.
  2. Money Maths Practice — answer questions on money arithmetic, percentages, discounts, profit & loss, budgeting, simple and compound interest, successive discounts, markup & margin, break-even and revenue. Every correct answer earns XP.
  3. Budget Planner — split your cash across inventory, rent, staff, utilities, marketing and an emergency reserve. The planner checks the numbers and shows what is left.
  4. Bank & Savings — move money into savings, then see the difference between simple and compound interest. This is a maths model only — no real money is involved.
  5. Business Expansion — buy upgrades (bigger shelves, faster checkout, a better supplier) and meet the reputation, days and profit targets to unlock the next business level.
  6. Tycoon Showdown — you and a rival answer the same questions each round. Highest score wins.
