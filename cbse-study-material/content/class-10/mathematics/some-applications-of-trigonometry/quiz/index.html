About the questions
14 of the 20 questions are genuine CBSE board previous year questions from the 2021, 2023 and 2024 board papers and carry the label CBSE PYQ · [year]. The remaining 6 questions are original items written by Vyasa Academy in the CBSE board pattern and carry the label ORIGINAL · CBSE EXAM-STYLE. All questions stay within the Some Applications of Trigonometry scope of this chapter: heights and distances solved with the line of sight, the angle of elevation, the angle of depression and the given trigonometric ratios.
