Class 10 Maths – Quadratic Equations Chapter Test

A timed, application-heavy chapter test for CBSE Class 10 Mathematics, Chapter 4 – Quadratic Equations. It combines authentic, year-labelled CBSE previous year questions with original application and competency problems, including factorisation, the quadratic formula, the discriminant and nature of roots, word problems, reasoning items and a case study, with instant auto-scoring, self-assessment and detailed solutions. All questions stay within the quadratic-equations scope of the syllabus.

General Instructions
  1. This is a timed test. You have 60 minutes to attempt all 21 questions. Warnings appear at 15 minutes, 5 minutes, 2 minutes and 1 minute; when the time is up the test submits automatically.
  2. Section A (Questions 1–8) and Section B (Questions 9–10) are auto-scored. Sections C to F (Questions 11–21) are open-ended: answer in the space given, then award yourself marks against the mark scheme in the review step.
  3. Use the question palette to jump to any question, the flag button to mark questions for later review, and Clear Response to remove an answer.
  4. Answers and detailed solutions are shown only after you submit the test.
  5. Verified previous year questions carry a CBSE PYQ badge with the exam year. Every other question is original and exam-style.
  6. Open-ended questions require working and reasoning, not just the final answer.
