This quiz is timed. You have 15 minutes to answer all 20 questions. Warnings appear at the 5, 2 and 1 minute marks, and the quiz submits automatically when time runs out.

About the questions
16 of the 20 questions are genuine CBSE board previous year questions from the 2020 to 2024 board papers and carry the label CBSE PYQ · [year]. The remaining 4 questions are original items written by Vyasa Academy in the CBSE board pattern and carry the label ORIGINAL · CBSE EXAM-STYLE. All questions stay within the Introduction to Trigonometry scope of this chapter: trigonometric ratios and their reciprocals, standard values at 0°, 30°, 45°, 60° and 90°, finding ratios when one ratio is given, and the identities \( \sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1 \), \( \sec^2\theta = 1 + \tan^2\theta \) and \( \mathrm{cosec}^2\theta = 1 + \cot^2\theta \).
