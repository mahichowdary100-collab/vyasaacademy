How to Prepare Calculus for Class 12 CBSE

By Mahendra Babu Chennupati | 27 Aug 2026 | 2 min read

On this page

  • Own differentiation before anything else
  • Build a technique bank for integration
  • Applications turn theory into marks
  • Differential equations: pattern and practice
  • The final weeks for calculus
  • The summary

In most recent Class 12 papers, calculus units account for a large share of the mathematics marks. That makes preparation order matter: work through differentiation fluently before integration, because integration leans on it. Here is how to structure the whole block.

Own differentiation before anything else

Build automatic differentiation of algebraic, trigonometric, exponential, and logarithmic functions. Practise chain, product, and quotient rules until you can apply the right rule without hesitation. Differentiation is the engine of calculus, and every later unit rewards this fluency.

Build a technique bank for integration

  1. Direct standard integrals first: get familiar with the basic list in the syllabus.
  2. Substitution: learn the patterns that signal it, such as recognising a function and its derivative together.
  3. By parts: practise the standard choices like logarithmic before algebraic before exponential.
  4. Partial fractions and trigonometric identities: learn when the structure signals them.

Keep an integration playbook: for each technique, one example problem that shows the signal for using it. When you are stuck on a question in the exam, scan the signals to pick the technique.

Applications turn theory into marks

Application of derivatives covers rate of change, increasing and decreasing behaviour, and maxima and minima, most of which become easy once differentiation is fluent. Application of integrals involves finding areas between curves, heavily dependent on neat diagrams and correct limits. Practise the figure first for every area problem, and the limits are far harder to get wrong.

Differential equations: pattern and practice

Separable variables, homogeneous, and linear differential equations are each a small repeated procedure. Practise ten of each type and the chapter becomes more mechanical than any other in calculus. Write the standard form before you start so you know which pattern you are applying.

The final weeks for calculus

  • Redo your differentiation rules and integration playbook from memory twice a week.
  • Solve two full differentiation and two full integration exercises under a timer.
  • Mark every slip by cause and redo only the patterns that gave trouble.
  • Leave no blank in a mock: a written partial attempt earns method marks.

The summary

Calculus rewards orderly preparation: fluent differentiation, a written technique bank for integration, diagrams for applications, and pattern practice for differential equations. Follow that order, practise on schedule, and calculus becomes the strongest part of the paper.
