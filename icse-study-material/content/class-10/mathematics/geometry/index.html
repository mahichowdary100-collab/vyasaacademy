Five Topics in This Unit

1. Similarity

Similar triangles, ratio of areas and the angle-bisector theorem.

2. Loci

Constructing and identifying simple loci.

3. Circles

Angle properties, cyclic quadrilaterals and arcs.

4. Tangents and Intersecting Chords

Tangent properties and chord products.

5. Constructions

Circumcircle, incircle and tangents to a circle.
