General Instructions
  1. This is a timed test. You have 60 minutes to attempt all 25 questions. When the time is up, the test submits automatically.
  2. Section A (Questions 1–10) is auto-scored. Section B (Questions 11–25) is open-ended: answer in the space given, then award yourself marks against the mark scheme in the review step.
  3. Use the question palette to jump to any question and the flag button to mark questions for later review.
  4. Answers and detailed solutions are shown only after you submit the test.
  5. Question sourcing: 10 of the 25 questions are verified ICSE board questions from the 2023 and 2024 papers, tagged ICSE Board. The remaining 15 are original exam-style questions created by Vyasa Academy. Where the original board question was an MCQ, the answer options in this test are original options.
  6. Open-ended questions require working and reasoning, not just the final answer.
